# Everyday Analogy Contract

> Historical compatibility reference only. All new invocations use Fable; do not
> use this document to select a mode or automatically downgrade a request.

Use for daily openings (version 4) and requests for a familiar life-based analogy.
Ordinary direct teaching still defaults to Bridge; explicitly requested complete
fables still use Fable. Delaying a concept name does not require a full fable.

## One complete relation, not a second subject to learn

Identify the one relation the reader needs before choosing a setting.
Use objects and actions the reader can already picture, not invented equipment
that needs its own instructions. A classroom, queue, room, lamp, or phone is an
example, not an approved vocabulary list. Do not mechanically replace a strange
word with a familiar noun if its action still invents an unsupported mechanism.

Tell enough to understand the initial situation, necessary constraint, action
or change, why the consequence follows, and the observable outcome.
Completeness refers to this selected relation, not every node of a paper.
An imagined situation may visualize a supported relationship without claiming
that its physical actions are the real research mechanism.

120–250 Chinese characters and 3–6 sentences are orientation only, never
acceptance limits. There is no total word, sentence, or paragraph ceiling,
and no mandatory two-paragraph background. Preserve necessary background,
conditions, transitions, and results even when the story is longer.
Delete decoration, repetition, and auxiliary devices only when understanding
survives. Do not rescue a missing causal link by shortening or by adding lore.

Prefer few mechanism-bearing objects. Three is a cognitive-load prompt, not
a counting gate; ordinary background nouns do not count. Keep an extra object
when it makes the selected relation easier rather than harder to understand.

## Return to fact

Use this reading order (headings may follow the delivery surface):

1. **生活画面:** familiar scene with a complete selected relation.
2. **一句话核心:** state the useful intuition with its necessary scope.
3. **真实概念对应:** reveal the name; map the important relation/actions and
   distinguish the visualization from the actual operation.
4. **边界纠正:** say what is omitted and which tempting inference is invalid.
   A few concrete boundaries are preferable to an unrelated disclaimer list.

For daily output, keep the concept and aliases out of the title and scene;
reveal them in the factual return. Do not load the full Fable vocabulary
firewall or invent substitute apparatus merely to conceal terminology.
Move technical derivations, source statistics, calibration, and uncertainty
into the factual explanation/example. A number needed for the familiar
action may remain; hypothetical quantities must not impersonate empirical data.

The technical example must explain the same relation but can use a different
setting and appropriate values. It must not reverse the operation, change the
claim's conditions, or turn the illustration into evidence. Preserve exact
source data and uncertainty when actually citing measurements.

## Semantic and comprehension review

Apply the shared evidence-authority and beginner-comprehension gates to the
selected relation, not to reconstruction of the entire paper.
A reader should be able to retell what happened and why without consulting a
dictionary of story words. That is a review criterion, not a claim of measured
reading time or demonstrated learner mastery.

Reject even a short draft if it introduces apparatus vocabulary, skips the
reason for a consequence, or teaches the wrong intuition. Accept necessary
length when the causal chain remains easy to follow. If repair cannot produce
a faithful analogy, narrow the relation; use the daily ranked-source fallback
for a required opening, or explain the limit and use Bridge for standalone work.

A boundary note cannot repair a false central analogy. In particular, test
whether returning to an allowed set is being confused with recovering prior
information, and whether indistinguishability under specified observations
is being exaggerated into indistinguishability under every possible test.
Domain-specific examples belong in regression cases, not universal rules.

For daily delivery, follow `daily-briefing-interface.md`. Structural validation
checks transport and field contracts; semantic review checks fidelity and
comprehension. Neither a script exit code nor matching a keyword proves these.
